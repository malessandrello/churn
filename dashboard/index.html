Methodology

How it was built

Data and cleaning

A dataset of 7,043 customers and 33 variables. Constant columns (country, state) were dropped, and blank total charges for brand-new customers were handled.

No data leakage

The churn score, CLTV and churn reason (which only exists for customers who already left) were excluded from the model, along with identifiers and fine-grained geography.

Modeling

Stratified 75/25 split and 5-fold cross-validation with caret. It was benchmarked against a Random Forest; logistic regression was chosen for interpretability, with similar performance.
